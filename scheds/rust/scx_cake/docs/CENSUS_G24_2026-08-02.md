## Verdicts and dispositions

1. **Pinned-wake preempt — DEAD, fifth zero** (3 G10 workloads + both arms).
   Runtime expense ≈ nil (cold branch behind a 2-insn guard), so deletion is
   the no-cold-paths law, not a frames win. **Disposition: delete — maintainer
   sign-off queued, no trade exists.**
2. **Sibling kick — dead in both regimes.** Same class, same disposition.
3. **SLEEPER_LAG syncgate — confirmed not a predicate** (98.6% / 95.5% pass).
   Simplification: make saturated convergence unconditional, drop the
   pretend-gate. Touches a 1.4M-fires/arm path → carries a game screen.
4. **Steal ring — the expense headline, NOT removable.** 93–98% of all
   dispatches walk the ring (up to 16 remote qmark probes) for a ~1% hit rate
   — but the hits are 4.5k steals/s of real work conservation. Disposition:
   keep; register an optimization hypothesis (per-LLC qmark summary word,
   §R.16 tension acknowledged) — candidate G25.
5. **Serial handoff — 0.01–0.03% fire in game regimes, evaluated on 100% of
   wakes, benchmark-critical** (mutex +44%). The GAMES-ONLY trade item, as
   §G10 already framed it. Maintainer call.
6. **Keep, working as designed:** qmark ordered admission (correctness role,
   ~free), qmark test-before-store (≥85% elided), G17 divert (heavily live —
   its 82%-of-enqueues rate is itself evidence toward the unexplained G17
   mechanism), R.6 (94.5% effective).
7. **Weak revert candidate:** G20 kthread arm (fires ~50/s, endpoint null,
   premise unstable). Simplicity-only.
